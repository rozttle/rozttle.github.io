Mission Statement

Project Minerva is a long-range exoplanetary research initiative focused on identifying a primitive, life-supporting environment beyond the solar system. Project Minerva’s objective includes the controlled revival of extinct keystone species whose absence has accelerated the degradation of Earth’s ecological systems. Aboard MINERVA ONE, a multidisciplinary team of astronauts and paleo-biologists transports preserved ancestral material to assess off-world viability, habitat compatibility, and long-term ecological resilience.

Guided by contemporary re-wilding theory, Project Minerva operates on the premise that restoring prehistoric biological networks may offer the only scientifically plausible pathway toward the recovery of Earth’s future ecosystems.
